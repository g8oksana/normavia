About

A young firm. Long experience.

Normavia Group is a Seattle-based quality and cybersecurity certification consultancy. We were founded in 2025 to bring decades of audit experience to companies pursuing ISO and cybersecurity certifications.

Our story

Built on the audit table

Normavia Group was founded in 2025 by experienced auditors and consultants who had spent twenty years inside quality and security programs — sometimes running them, sometimes auditing them, often building them from scratch.

The premise is simple: companies pursuing ISO 9001, ISO 27001, ISO 13485, and CMMC don’t need another consultancy that hands them a binder. They need a partner who has been on both sides of the audit and knows what passes and what doesn’t.

We’re based in Seattle and work with clients across the United States — mostly remote, with on-site audits and management reviews when they matter.

How we work

The three things we ask our clients to expect from us, every engagement.

Honest scoping

If you don’t need certification, we’ll say so. If your timeline is unrealistic, we’ll say that too. The first conversation is always free.

Real expertise

You’ll work with people who have led 300+ audits across 22 countries — not a junior assigned a checklist.

Long partnership

Certification is the start, not the finish. Most of our clients work with us across multiple recertification cycles.
