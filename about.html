About

Compliance guidance built for business momentum.

Norma Via Group helps quality, environmental, safety, and cybersecurity teams get audit-ready faster — with practical guidance that supports certification, customer trust, and growth.

Why Norma Via

Experienced guidance without the guesswork.

The premise is simple: companies pursuing ISO 9001, ISO 14001, ISO 45001, ISO 27001, ISO 42001, ISO 20000-1, ISO 13485, and CMMC don’t need another consultancy that hands them a binder. They need a partner who understands compliance from three angles: manufacturer, auditor, and consultant.

We help teams prioritize what matters, close gaps efficiently, and prepare for certification with clear deliverables, realistic timelines, and hands-on support.

We’re based in Seattle and work with clients across the United States — mostly remote, with on-site audits and management reviews when they matter.
