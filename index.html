How we work

Every engagement follows the same four-step path — tailored, not formulaic.

  1. 1

    Gap analysis

    Where you are versus where the standard says you need to be — clear, prioritized, no fluff.

  2. 2

    Implementation & documentation

    We build the management system with you — processes, records, training, controls.

  3. 3

    Internal audit & CAPA

    We run the dress rehearsal: audit your system, find the issues, drive corrective actions.

  4. 4

    Certification audit support

    We’re in the room — preparing your team, answering auditor questions, closing findings.
