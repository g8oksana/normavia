What it is

The aerospace quality standard built specifically for MRO

AS9110 is the IAQG quality management system standard tailored to aviation Maintenance, Repair and Overhaul organizations. While AS9100 is built around manufacturing, AS9110 adds MRO-specific requirements for airworthiness, maintenance documentation, regulatory compliance, and the return-to-service process.

Certification is increasingly required by airline customers, aircraft manufacturers, and defense primes who want confidence that their aircraft and components are maintained under a controlled, auditable quality system — not just a regulatory certificate.

Who needs AS9110?

Aviation MRO providers

  • Line, base, and component maintenance organizations
  • Engine overhaul facilities
  • Avionics repair stations seeking customer-required certification

Defense maintenance contractors

  • Military aircraft and equipment maintenance under DCAA oversight
  • Government contractors subject to AS9110 flow-down from primes
  • Organizations transitioning from ISO 9001 to aerospace-specific standards

Multi-standard organizations

  • FAA Part 145 repair stations adding AS9110 to their approval
  • Organizations holding AS9100 seeking MRO scope expansion
  • MROs responding to customer audit findings requiring OASIS registration
