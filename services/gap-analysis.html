What it is

Your current state versus the standard — no guessing

A gap analysis is an independent, structured comparison of what you do today against what an ISO standard or regulatory framework requires. It tells you what’s already in place, what’s partially there, and what’s missing entirely.

Done right, a gap analysis is the most valuable day you spend on a certification project. Done wrong — or skipped — it’s the main reason certification projects stall, overspend, or fail the first audit.

Who needs a gap analysis?

Companies starting certification

  • About to begin ISO 9001, 27001, 13485, or any other standard
  • Need a realistic timeline and budget before committing
  • Want to know what to tackle first

Organizations preparing for audit

  • Surveillance or recertification audit is approaching
  • Internal team not confident about current compliance state
  • New management needs an honest system assessment

Teams after a failed or difficult audit

  • Received major nonconformances and need to understand why
  • Want an independent view before the next registrar visit
  • Looking to strengthen the system, not just patch findings

Our approach to gap analysis

Structured, evidence-based, and blunt about what we find.

  1. 1

    Document review

    We review your existing policies, procedures, records, and previous audit reports before setting foot on-site.

  2. 2

    Process interviews

    On-site or remote interviews with process owners, managers, and frontline staff to test whether documents reflect reality.

  3. 3

    Clause-by-clause assessment

    Every requirement of the standard is assessed as Conforming, Partially Conforming, or Nonconforming — with evidence.

  4. 4

    Findings report & roadmap

    A written report with findings ranked by risk, a prioritized remediation plan, and a realistic timeline to certification.

What you get

Gap analysis report

Clause-by-clause findings in plain English. Conforming, partial, and missing — each with supporting evidence.

Risk-ranked findings

Not everything is equal. We rank gaps by audit risk so your team knows what to fix first.

Remediation roadmap

A phased action plan: who owns what, what resources are needed, and a realistic timeline to close each gap.

Effort & cost estimate

Honest scope of work to complete implementation, so you can budget and resource the project correctly.

Executive summary

One-page summary for leadership: readiness percentage, top risks, and go/no-go recommendation for audit scheduling.

Debrief session

Live walkthrough of findings with your team. Questions answered, priorities agreed, next steps clear.
