What it is

Industry-managed accreditation for aerospace-critical processes

NADCAP (National Aerospace and Defense Contractors Accreditation Program) is managed by the Performance Review Institute (PRI) and mandated by major aerospace primes — Boeing, Airbus, Lockheed Martin, Raytheon, and others — for suppliers performing special processes on aerospace parts and assemblies.

Special processes are manufacturing operations where the process itself, not just the finished product, must be controlled — because defects in the process may not be detectable in the finished part. NADCAP accreditation provides aerospace customers a single, industry-wide audit instead of individual customer audits for every prime you supply.

Special process categories we support

Thermal & chemical processing

  • Heat treatment (AC7102 checklist)
  • Chemical processing — anodize, plating, conversion coating
  • Shot peening and surface treatment

Joining & fabrication

  • Welding — fusion, resistance, and electron beam (AC7110)
  • Brazing and soldering for aerospace applications
  • Composite material fabrication and processing

Inspection & testing

  • Non-Destructive Testing — PT, MT, RT, UT, ET (AC7114)
  • Measurement systems and laboratory accreditation
  • Fluid distribution and electrical / electronic systems

Our NADCAP preparation approach

Checklist-driven, technically precise, and calibrated to the specific commodity your audit covers.

  1. 1

    Checklist gap assessment

    We work through the applicable NADCAP audit criteria checklist (e.g., AC7102 for heat treatment) line-by-line against your current process controls, equipment qualifications, and records.

  2. 2

    Process & procedure remediation

    We help close specific gaps: procedure updates, equipment qualification documentation, operator certification records, and process parameter controls — targeted to the audit checklist.

  3. 3

    Mock audit

    Full mock NADCAP audit using the actual PRI checklist before the scheduled accreditation audit — identifies any remaining findings so they can be resolved before the auditor arrives.

  4. 4

    Audit support & CAPA

    We support the PRI audit, manage nonconformance responses, and prepare corrective action plans that PRI and your subscribing customers will accept.
