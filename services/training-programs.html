What it is

Training from people who do this work every day

ISO certification without trained people is just paper. Your internal auditors need to know how to actually audit — not just complete a checklist. Your process owners need to understand why the requirements exist, not just what the words say. Your management team needs to know what “management review” actually means in practice.

Our training is delivered by consultants who run real audits in real facilities. Every example, case study, and exercise comes from the field — not from a slide deck written in a conference room.

Training programs we offer

ISO Internal Auditor Training

  • ISO 9001, ISO 13485, ISO 27001, ISO 14001, ISO 45001
  • Audit planning, conducting, reporting, and CAPA follow-up
  • Hands-on practice with real audit scenarios

QMS Awareness Training

  • What the standard requires and why it matters to your team
  • Role-based training: management, process owners, frontline staff
  • Customized to your industry, product, and QMS documentation

RCA / CAPA Workshop

  • 5 Whys, Fishbone, and Fault Tree Analysis in practice
  • CAPA documentation that satisfies FDA and ISO auditors
  • Exercises using real nonconformances from your own system

Lead Auditor Preparation

  • Preparation for third-party lead auditor certification courses
  • Audit management, opening/closing meetings, audit report writing
  • Auditor decision-making and finding classification

Regulatory Awareness (FDA / MDSAP)

  • 21 CFR Part 820 / QMSR requirements for medical device teams
  • MDSAP audit process and grading model explained
  • What FDA investigators look for in an inspection

Custom Programs

  • Training designed around your specific QMS, processes, and gaps
  • On-site, live-online, or hybrid delivery
  • Available as one-day workshops or multi-day programs

Our approach to training

Practical, engaging, and built to change behavior — not just pass a knowledge check.

  1. 1

    Needs assessment

    We identify knowledge gaps, current competency levels, and role-specific requirements before building the curriculum.

  2. 2

    Customized curriculum

    Training content tailored to your standard, industry, QMS documentation, and real-world examples from your own processes.

  3. 3

    Delivery & practice

    Live instruction combined with hands-on exercises — audit practice, CAPA simulations, or documentation reviews using real materials.

  4. 4

    Competency verification

    Post-training assessment and a documented training record suitable for your QMS and registrar review.
